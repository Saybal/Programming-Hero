Frequently   Asked Questions

1. How can I start learning English on this website?
Easy 😃.Just pick up our lessons one by one sequentially. Our classes are designed based on beginner to advanced levels. Now, it is your responsibility to be disciplined, patient, and productive. You should learn at least 5 new words from our lessons and try to apply them in your daily language and this is the best way of learning because you can apply your knowledge practically. Finally, you have no choice without passing through a hard way during your lessons but never give up. Only this can bring you success 🤞.
2. Is this website free to use?
Yes, this website is Completely free. We have no paid section here. So, you can feel easy to go through our all sections and your success is our honorarium ❤️.
3. Do I need to create an account?
Yes, you have to create an account to your learning journey in our website. Why? Because it will help us to track your progress and provide proper suggestions to improve your learning 💡.
4. How can I build my English vocabulary?
We have discussed it slightly in our first question. To elaborate, you can choose a word from any of our lessons and search in google how to and where to use this word in daily life? Or you can also search movie clips by thise word. This helps to realise the expressions of this word. These things you can add to vocabulary
5. Do you offer certificates for completed courses?
Yes, we offer certificates for completed courses. After successfully finishing a course, you can download and share your certificate to showcase your achievement.
